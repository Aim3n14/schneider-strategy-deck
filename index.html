Schneider ElectricVision & mission

How far is Schneider
from its 2030 purpose?

Climate and access are past halfway; efficiency, the core of the mission, is only at 9% of its target.

Vision
“Build a more sustainable and resilient future, for everyone.”

Emissions, energy access, skills and design turn a broad vision into outcomes we can count.

Mission
“Be the customer’s energy technology partner for efficiency and sustainability, through electrification, automation and digital intelligence.”

Energy saved is what customers get, software shows it, trained people deliver it.
